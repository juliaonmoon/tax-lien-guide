Key difference from tax liens: a tax-deed auction is about acquiring the property (or a defeasible/redeemable interest in it), not merely earning certificate interest. Some states transfer a deed with no normal post-sale redemption; others, such as Texas and Georgia, give the former owner a statutory redemption period after the sale.
Educational research only, not legal or investment advice. Tax deed rules are county- and state-specific. Verify title, surviving liens, occupancy, redemption rights, payment deadlines, property condition, access, environmental issues, HOA obligations, and local sale terms before bidding.
