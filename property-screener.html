Tax Deed Property Screener — Prototype

Live prototype using two counties in two states: Brevard County, Florida and Tarrant County, Texas. The system pulls official sale listings daily, normalizes the parcel/account records, and gives each row an explainable research-priority score. Missing fields stay visibly blank instead of being guessed.

Score meaning: this is a research queue, not a recommendation to bid. The prototype gives credit for things like an official opening bid and current sale status, while penalizing missing value data and unreviewed title/liens. Before any real-world decision, independently verify title, surviving liens, redemption rules, occupancy, zoning, condition, access, HOA/municipal obligations and actual market value.
Prototype collector uses public official county pages only. It does not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, robots restrictions or other access controls. Brevard currently provides richer auction-list data than Tarrant, so the Texas rows may initially have fewer fields until the enrichment adapter is added.
